Realmwright

A village, a wilderness, and five rival crowns that never sleep.

You rule a small settlement at the edge of an unmapped continent. Grow it into a kingdom by farming, building, trading, scheming and fighting. The other realms don't wait for you: they build, ally and go to war with each other whether you're watching or not.

DiplomacyBe allied with every surviving realm for one full year.
