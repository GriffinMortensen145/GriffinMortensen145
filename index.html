Sounding

The lift cable snapped and you're alone at the top of a cave system nobody has mapped. It's completely dark down here, and the things that live here hunt by sound.

Your scanner sends out a pulse that shows the walls for a moment. Anything nearby hears it too. Dig deeper, build better gear, and find out what's at the bottom.
