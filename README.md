# Games

Two browser games, each a single HTML file with no build step.

- **Sounding**, an echo-scanning cave roguelike: https://griffinmortensen145.github.io/GriffinMortensen145/
- **Realmwright**, a kingdom-building strategy game: https://griffinmortensen145.github.io/GriffinMortensen145/realm/

---

## Sounding

A 2D mining and survival roguelike that runs in the browser. You're a miner cut off at the top of a procedurally generated cave system. It's pitch dark, and the creatures down here hunt by sound. Your scanner sends out echo pulses that show the walls for a few seconds, but every pulse can be heard.

**Play:** https://griffinmortensen145.github.io/GriffinMortensen145/

### How it plays

- **Echo scanning.** Space sends a pulse. Quiet (1) shows a small area and barely makes noise, Normal (2) shows more, and Powerful (3) lights up a huge area and wakes everything nearby.
- **Noise and light.** Footsteps, digging, fighting and pulses are all noise. Crawlers and burrowers track sound, shriekers and stalkers are drawn to your headlamp, and shriekers scream to call others when they spot you. Shift lets you sneak silently.
- **Mining.** Dig through rock for copper, iron, quartz and cobalt. Hard strata separate each zone, so you need a better pickaxe to get through.
- **Stations.** Build stations to recharge, heal, bank ore and craft. Creatures won't come near them. If you die, you're recalled to your deepest station, which burns out, and the ore you were carrying is lost. With no station, the run is over.
- **Crafting.** Upgrade your pickaxe, scanner, headlamp, battery, armor and ore pack, and make ladders, glowsticks, decoy chimes, stun charges and medkits.
- **Five zones.** Upper tunnels, deep stone, crystal hollows, the magma deep, and the hollow at the bottom, where something is waiting.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D | Move (Shift to sneak) |
| W / S | Jump, climb ladders |
| Space | Echo pulse |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Quiet, normal or powerful pulse |
| Left mouse | Dig, attack |
| Right mouse | Place selected item |
| Q or mouse wheel | Change selected item |
| F | Headlamp on or off |
| G | Throw decoy chime |
| E | Stun charge |
| R | Medkit |
| C | Craft (at a station) |
| Esc | Pause |
| M | Mute |

Every run uses a new seed. Type a seed on the title screen to replay a specific cave.

## Realmwright

You rule a small medieval settlement on a procedurally generated continent. Grow it into a kingdom through farming, building, trade, diplomacy and war. Three to six rival realms run the same economy and make their own decisions, so they found cities, form alliances and fight each other whether or not you're involved. The chronicle in the corner reports what's happening across the continent.

### How it plays

- **City building.** Farms, houses, lumber camps, quarries, iron and gold mines, markets, libraries and barracks go on tiles inside your borders. Cities grow from village to town to castle and can be walled.
- **Economy.** Food, wood, stone, iron, gold and population. Buildings need workers, so houses matter as much as production.
- **Expansion.** Found new cities to claim land. Scouts explore the wilderness and make contact with other realms.
- **Diplomacy.** Alliances, peace, trade routes, gifts, and buying or selling resources. Allies expect you to join their wars.
- **Battles.** Command battles in real time or auto-resolve them. Infantry beat cavalry, cavalry beat archers and siege, archers beat infantry, and siege engines break walls. Formations: line, wedge and block.
- **Research.** Four branches (agriculture, construction, military, economics) across three ages: Settlement, Expansion and Empire.
- **Victory.** Conquest (60% of cities), economy (25,000 gold), diplomacy (a year allied with every surviving realm) or knowledge (the Enlightenment). A rival can win the same way.

### Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Drag, WASD | Pan the map |
| Scroll | Zoom |
| Click | Select a tile, city or army |
| Right-click | Move the selected army, or attack an enemy city |
| Space | Pause |
| T / G / V | Research, realms, victory progress |
| In battle: drag | Select companies |
| In battle: right-click | Move or attack |
| In battle: Q, C, H | Select all, charge, hold |
| In battle: 1, 2, 3 | Line, wedge, block formation |
